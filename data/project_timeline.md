# Project timeline 2024-2025

Internal record of the delivery, hiring and go-to-market milestones behind the reported
quarters. Fictional company, fictional projects: use it to explain movements in the
financial files, not as a source of figures.

## Narrative

**2024 — build the second product.** The year opened with a single revenue line, the Core
Platform, and a stated goal of shipping a second one. The Analytics Suite went to private
beta in March and to general availability in June, which is why its revenue is a small
slice of the first half and a visibly larger one from Q3 onward. An autumn
demand-generation push, the largest campaign the company had run, landed in Q4 alongside a
sales hiring wave — both quarters of marketing and salary spend move with it.

**2025 — widen the platform.** Q1 is the softest quarter of the two years: enterprise
renewals cluster in January, several slipped into February, and the autumn campaign had
already pulled some deals forward into 2024Q4. The Integrations marketplace opened in
April, the EMEA data region came out of migration in July, and Core Platform v4 shipped in
October ahead of the year's biggest campaign. Q3 and Q4 are the two strongest quarters on
record, in that order.
